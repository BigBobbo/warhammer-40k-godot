## Army rules (every Space Marine army: Space Marines and every chapter)

### Combat Doctrines
At the start of your Command phase, you can select one combat doctrine listed below. If you do, that combat doctrine is active for friendly ADEPTUS ASTARTES units with this ability until the start of your next Command phase. ASSAULT DOCTRINE When this unit is selected to make an advance move , that advance move does not prevent this unit from being eligible to declare a charge . DEVASTATOR DOCTRINE This unit’s ranged attacks have [ASSAULT] . TACTICAL DOCTRINE When this unit is selected to make a fall-back move , that fall-back move does not prevent this unit from being eligible to shoot and eligible to declare a charge . Unless otherwise stated: You can only select each combat doctrine once per battle. Only one combat doctrine can be active for each unit. If a rule makes a combat doctrine active for a unit, any combat doctrine previously active for that unit is no longer active for that unit.

### Transhuman Strategist
At the start of the battle round, if a model with this ability is your WARLORD , gain 1CP.

### Librarius
ADEPTUS ASTARTES PSYKER units with this ability have a psyker level of 1 or higher, specified in that unit’s abilities. Each psychic ability has a psychic level of 1 or higher, specified in that ability’s name. In a battle round, a friendly ADEPTUS ASTARTES PSYKER unit can use a number of psychic abilities whose total psychic level does not exceed that PSYKER unit’s psyker level . Example: In a battle round, a psyker level 3 PSYKER unit could use three psychic level 1 abilities, or one psychic level 1 ability and one psychic level 2 ability, or one psychic level 3 ability.
