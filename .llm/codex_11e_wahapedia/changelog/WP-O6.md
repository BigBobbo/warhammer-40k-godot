# WP-O6 — Codex: Orks datasheet abilities (player-facing bullets)

- Every Ork datasheet ability now follows the 11th-edition Codex: Orks text (see `ork_abilities_audit.md` for the full list). The old 10th-edition abilities those datasheets no longer have (Mekaniak, Sawbonez, Grot Oiler, Grot Riggers, Distraction Grot, Shooty Power Trip, Da Biggest and da Best, Splat!, Drive-by Dakka, Hold Still and Say 'Aargh!' and the rest) are gone, together with their pop-ups.
- Abilities you choose to use appear as free "Ability" cards in the same pop-up as stratagems, at the moment the rule says: Boss' Ammo Runt, Ammo Runts and Pulsa Rokkit when a unit is selected to shoot; Rokkit Barrage, Squig Barrage, Bust 'Em and Madcap Artillery after a unit has shot (picked automatically when only one enemy unit was hit); Intimidating Motivation, Get Back in da Race!, Keep Huntin'!, Clever Know-wotz, Found 'Em!, Drive-by Skorchin' and Shokk-boosta when a unit is selected to move; Bomb Squigs, Bomb Squig and Gut Hatch after a normal move; Makari, Hoist Dat Banner! and Da Jump at the start of the Movement phase; Supreme Kunnin' when an enemy ends a move near Nazdreg; Rokkit Charge and Warpath when a unit is selected to fight; Krushin' Impetus after a charge; Kunnin' Infiltrator, Aerial Manoover, Burna Bomb and Boom Bomb at the end of your opponent's Fight phase; Crude Surgery (with Catch Dat Red Bit), Fix Dat Armour Up, Push Dat Bit Back In and Full Throttle in your Battle-shock step; That'll Learn Ya when Gretchin become battle-shocked.
- If several abilities apply to the same moment (for example Boss' Ammo Runt and Ammo Runts on a Warboss-led mob), the pop-up comes back after each use until you decline.
- Abilities that need a target open a picker listing only legal units (e.g. Pulsa Rokkit lists enemy Monsters/Vehicles within 24").
- New passive rules in combat: Might Is Right (+3 A / +2 S after charging), Tide of Muscle, Paired Krumpas, Arrogant Invulnerability, Krumpin' Time, Kustom Force Field (4+ invulnerable vs shooting), More Dakka / Even More Dakka, Kustom Dakka, Nazdreg's Know-wotz, Prophet of da Great Waaagh! (aura), Strafing Run, Go fer da Weak Spot, Deff from Above, Mobile Arsenal, Big an' Shooty / Big an' Stompy, Spiteful Power Trip, Walking Bastion, Blitz Dem Gitz!, Fully Loaded Butchery, Finderz Keeperz, High-speed Carnage, Brutal Impact, Dodge Dis!, Sumfin' to Prove, Da Bigger Dey Iz, Hardy Bioniks, Dread 'Ard, Mobile Fortress, Blastajet Force Field, Super Runts, Nowhere to Hide. They show in the dice log's modifier list by name.
- Movement and charges: Waaagh! Banner, Trophy Hunters and Thundering Stampede add to charge rolls; Fuel-mixa Grot adds to advance rolls; Runnin' wiv da Herd gives +4" Move when the Painboss joins a mounted unit; Madcap Artillery's "smashed" is -2" Move and "grounded" stops a unit taking to the skies.
- Ghazghkull, Boss Snikrot... characters with "Lone Operative while near friends" (Da Grand Warlord's Ladz, Support Shokka, Boss of da Hunt, Beast Snagga Following, Speednob Support) get it only while the condition holds.
- Mobbed: after Beast Snagga Boyz charge, enemy Monsters and Vehicles they reach take a battle-shock roll at -1 (-2 with 13+ models). One Last Kill lets Mozrog fight on death on a 2+.
- Gretchin: Downtrodden (two Gretchin per transport space) and Thievin' Scavengers (secure an objective they control at the end of your Movement phase). Boyz can start actions while engaged (Never Too Busy to Fight). The Stompa (Waaagh! Effigy) and Zodgrod's Gretchin (Super Runts) are always riled up; units leaving the Stompa are riled up (Idol of Belligerence).
- Kommandos' Taktikal Know-wotz splits the unit at deployment (one half keeps Bomb Squig, the other gets Found 'Em!).
- Tramplasquig Charge: Crushing Impact costs 1 CP less on the Hunta Rig.
- AI: computer-controlled Orks use these abilities when they help (attack bonuses, mortal wounds, heals, riling up) and never stall a pop-up.
- Not yet in the game: Deff from the Shadows, Pilin' Out, Pulse Jet (selectable in Full Throttle but it does nothing yet), the Big'ed Bossbunka's Raucous Occupants / Fortification / Ramshackle Cover, and the keyword swap half of Runnin' wiv da Herd.
