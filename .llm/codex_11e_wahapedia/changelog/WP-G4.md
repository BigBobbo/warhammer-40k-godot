# WP-G4 — Advanced Firepower; Big'ed Bossbunka (player-facing bullets)

- Advanced Firepower (Caladius Annihilator) now works: when its ranged attacks target a Monster or Vehicle it can re-roll one hit roll, one wound roll and one damage roll each phase. When you pause on the dice, the resolution panel offers "ADVANCED FIREPOWER (free) — click a die to re-roll it"; on Fast Roll, the AI and Fire Overwatch the re-roll is made automatically (no hit re-roll for Fire Overwatch). Every re-roll is listed in the Game Log.
- Big'ed Bossbunka — Fortification: an enemy unit engaged only with Fortifications can now be shot (except with Blast weapons), at -1 to hit unless the weapon is Close-Quarters; the dice panel names "Fortification". If that unit falls back while not battle-shocked, its hazard rolls automatically pass.
- Big'ed Bossbunka — Raucous Occupants: while the Bossbunka is on your home objective it adds the OC of the models embarked inside it (battle-shocked passengers add nothing).
- Big'ed Bossbunka — Ramshackle Cover: a unit partly hidden behind the Bossbunka from the shooter gets the benefit of cover (its attacker's BS is worsened by 1; shown as "(cover)" on the hit roll).
- Fix: clicking a die to re-roll it in the shooting resolution panel no longer logs an engine error.
