# WP-C7 — Codex: Adeptus Custodes datasheet abilities (player-facing bullets)

- Every datasheet and wargear ability on the 29 Codex: Adeptus Custodes (11th edition) datasheets now does what its card says (Assault Vehicle and Advanced Firepower excepted — see the last bullet).
- Abilities you choose to use appear as free "Ability" cards in the stratagem window at the moment they apply:
  - **Start of any phase:** Trajann's **Moment Shackle** (once per battle: Watcher's Axe +3 A or a 3+ invulnerable save until the end of the phase), the Venatari's **Neutronium Cascade Mine** (once per battle: D3 mortal wounds to an enemy unit within 3", 2D3 against vehicles and monsters) and the Blade Champion's **Sword of the Throne** (pick its mark at the start of the first battle round, and again when the mark dies: +1 to wound against it).
  - **Your Movement phase:** **Archeotech Teleport-shunter** (Shield-Captain in Allarus Terminator Armour, once per battle): back into strategic reserves — the unit must arrive again this phase before you can end it.
  - **Your Shooting phase:** **Purity of Execution** (Prosecutors: a visible enemy within 18" is detected, +3" detection range), **Sanctified Flames** (Witchseekers: a unit they hit takes a battle-shock roll, -1 for Psykers), **Mobile Hunter** (Pallas: a D6" move after shooting, no charge this turn).
  - **Your opponent's Shooting phase:** **Vengeful Surge** (Shield-Captain, once per battle: a D6+2" surge move after it lost a wound) and **Repulsor Suspensor Technology** (Coronus, once per phase: a D6" move after it lost a wound). The move panel lets you drag the unit or use Auto-move.
  - **End of your opponent's Fight phase:** **Take Wing** (Venatari: into strategic reserves) and **Infernus Firebombs** (Ares: mortal wounds on 6s, and your shooting at that unit ignores cover until the end of your next turn).
  - **Swift Ruin:** Rapid Ingress on the Venatari with Verutum Lances costs 1 CP less and ignores other Rapid Ingress uses that phase; the Rapid Ingress dialog shows "Arrive (0 CP — Swift Ruin)" and offers the unit even when you have no CP left.
- Passive abilities apply automatically and are named in the dice log: Captain-General (re-roll hit and wound rolls of 1 within 6" of Trajann), Praesidium Shield (**-1 Damage for attacks allocated to the Shield-Captain**, shown as "Praesidium Shield: -1 Damage (3 -> 2)"; on Sentinel Guard: -1 to wound against stronger attacks), Vexilla (+1 OC; harmful Leadership modifiers are ignored), Living Fortress, Slayer of Tyrants, Quicksilver Execution, Unyielding Ancient, Destructor Optics, Guardian Eternal (ranged attacks only), Assault Dropship, Swift Onslaught, Seeker's Instincts, Corner the Quarry, Impenetrable Defence, Stand Vigil, Dread Foe, Death Blow, Deft Parry, Reap a Terrible Tally, Strike from the Skies; Sweeping Advance now works for any unit that was eligible to fight; Trajann must be your Warlord.
- The old 10th-edition versions are gone: the Fight-phase-only Moment Shackle dialog, Sentinel Storm (shoot again), Strategic Mastery, Martial Inspiration, Slayers of Tyrants, Devoted to Destruction, the Quicksilver Execution fly-over mortal wounds, the automatic Sanctified Flames test, and the 10e Captain-General / Dread Foe / Purity of Execution / Advanced Firepower rules.
- AI: the computer uses the start-of-phase abilities, Sanctified Flames and Infernus Firebombs when they help, and never leaves an ability card waiting.
- Not yet in the game: Assault Vehicle (the core rules have no "shock disembark move") and Advanced Firepower (re-roll one hit, one wound and one damage roll); their ability descriptions say so.
