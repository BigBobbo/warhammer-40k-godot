# WP-C3 — Codex: Adeptus Custodes army rules (player-facing bullets)

- Martial Ka'tah is now the Codex version: at the start of your Command phase every Custodes unit with Martial Ka'tah becomes **readied** (a gold "K" badge on its models; the hover card says "Readied").
- A readied unit can use ONE ka'tah ability, offered as a free "KA'TAH" card in the stratagem pop-up at the right moment: Conservai (your Command phase), Calistus (when selected to move), Salvus (when selected to shoot), Dacatarai / Kaptaris (start of the Fight phase), Rendax (when selected to fight). Using it removes the badge; a stratagem can still follow.
- Calistus: normal, advance and fall-back moves can pass through any model, Monsters and Vehicles included.
- Salvus: the unit ignores penalties to its BS, hit rolls and wound rolls this phase (shown as "Salvus (Ka'tah)" in the dice log).
- Dacatarai: melee attacks re-roll hit rolls of 1 against non-Monster/Vehicle units. Kaptaris: melee attacks against the unit are -1 to hit. Rendax: melee attacks gain [LETHAL HITS] against Monsters and Vehicles. All three work in your opponent's Fight phase too if the unit is still readied.
- Conservai: until the end of the turn the unit can start actions while engaged or battle-shocked, and can still shoot after starting one.
- Master of Ka'tahs (Shield-Captains): once per battle round a unit that is no longer readied can still use a ka'tah through the card's "Master of Ka'tahs" button.
- The old 10e Ka'tah stance pop-up (Dacatarai / Rendax at the start of each fight) is gone.
- Aegis of the Emperor: Custodes units get Feel No Pain 5+ against every kind of mortal wound (Devastating Wounds, Hazardous, Deadly Demise, stratagems...). The best Feel No Pain applies, never two.
- Daughters of the Abyss: Anathema Psykana units have Feel No Pain 3+ against psychic attacks, and Custodes units within 6" of them get Feel No Pain 5+ against psychic attacks (Psychic Null).
- Aquila Commander: if your Warlord has it, you gain 1CP at the start of each battle round (within the 1-bonus-CP-per-round limit); the game log says so.
- Anathema Psykana models (Witchseekers, Prosecutors, Vigilators, Knight-Centura...) can no longer be chosen as your Warlord; the army builder warns about it.
- AI: Custodes AI players use ka'tahs when they clearly help (Rendax against vehicles, Dacatarai / Kaptaris in combat, Calistus to fall back).
