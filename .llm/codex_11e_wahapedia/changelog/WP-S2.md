# WP-S2 — Codex: Space Marines (11e), Space Wolves army rules, detachments, enhancements

- Combat Doctrines: Space Wolves pick a doctrine each Command phase from a new COMBAT DOCTRINES block in the Command panel (Assault: advance and still charge; Devastator: ranged weapons are [ASSAULT]; Tactical: fall back and still shoot and charge). Each doctrine once per battle; it lasts until your next Command phase; the Game Log says what is active. The AI picks one too.
- Gladius Task Force in a Space Wolves army: Codex Discipline lets you pick one doctrine a second time.
- Transhuman Strategist: if Logan Grimnar or Ragnar Blackmane is your Warlord, you gain 1CP at the start of each battle round.
- Curse of the Wulfen: Wulfen near a Space Wolves Character (6") or a Wolf Priest (12") get +1 OC (+3 for Wulfen Dreadnoughts), so they can hold objectives; Wulfen take two transport spaces each.
- Sons of Russ: an Apothecary in a Space Wolves army is flagged as illegal.
- Champions of Fenris: Heroic Intervention on a Space Marine Character unit costs 1CP less (once per round per unit); the Heroic Intervention window shows each unit's real cost.
- Saga of the Beastslayer: Blood Claws, Thunderwolf Cavalry and Wulfen get [LETHAL HITS] against Characters, Monsters and Vehicles.
- Saga of the Great Wolf: Master of Wolves (+1 advance and charge with Assault, re-roll ranged hit rolls of 1 with Devastator, [SUSTAINED HITS 1] within 9" with Tactical) and Logan Grimnar's once-per-battle Howling Onslaught button; armies with other Chapters' units are flagged.
- Enhancements: Preyslayer (free charge re-roll), A Giant Amongst Giants (+2 W, +1 S in melee), Wolf-Touched (+2" Move, can lead Wulfen), Howlmaw (start of the Fight phase: an enemy within 6" tests battle-shock at -1), Grimnar's Mark (cheaper Heroic Intervention / Rapid Ingress even after another use), Skjald's Foretelling (melee [LANCE]); Hunter's Guile and Chariots of the Storm redeploy units (AI/engine only — the redeploy step still has no buttons).
- Fix: in 11th edition, a unit that may fall back and still shoot can now actually pick a shooting type after falling back.
