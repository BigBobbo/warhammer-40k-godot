# WP-O34 — Codex: Orks (11e) army rules + 15 detachment rules

Player-facing changes (11th edition games; 10th-edition games are unchanged):

- **Waaagh!**: Ork units with the Waaagh! ability get a FREE, optional re-roll of their advance roll (the re-roll dialog is branded "Waaagh!" and costs no CP).
- **War Cry** can be used at the start of *either* player's Command phase: in the opponent's Command phase the Ork player is offered WAR CRY in a free rule window. Riled up now lasts "until the end of the next turn" (a War Cry in the opponent's Command phase lasts through your next turn).
- **Da Boss**: at the start of each battle round, if your WARLORD has Da Boss you gain 1CP (Game Log entry).
- **Unstable Energies**: an Ork PSYKER's psychic abilities in a battle round cannot exceed its psyker level; an over-budget ability (Da Jump, Roar of Mork, Visions of Violence) is not offered and the Game Log + a toast say why. The budget resets each battle round.
- **War Horde — Get Stuck In**: Ork melee attacks have [SUSTAINED HITS 1].
- **Madcap Meks — Unpredictable Genius**: when all of a Big Mek / Mek Gunz / Morkanaut / Wazbom Blastajet unit's shots go at one unit, a D6 picks Dat's Weird (4+ invuln), Hop Splat (D3 mortal wounds on another enemy within 3" — you pick when there are several), Seekerz (+1 to hit), Lifted (FLY, no cover) or Runtified (-1 T).
- **Wurrband — Powers of da Waaagh!**: Ork PSYKER [PSYCHIC] attacks get +1 S (+1 per 5 models); Ork PSYKERs can use ROAR OF MORK at the end of your Movement phase and VISIONS OF VIOLENCE (Fights First) at the start of the Fight phase, through free rule windows.
- **Runt Swarm — Sneaky Little Gitz**: Gretchin are BATTLELINE and can still start an action after advancing or falling back.
- **Wreckas — Wreckin' and Lootin'**: Breaka Boyz / Flash Gitz / Tankbustas re-roll hit rolls of 1 in your Shooting phase when they or their target are within range of an objective.
- **Brute Bosses — 'Ard as Nails**: a Warboss destroyed in the Fight phase before it was selected to fight stays on a 3+ (+1 riled up) and fights before it is removed.
- **Bully Boyz — Displays of Savagery**: Nobz and Meganobz melee attacks have [SUSTAINED HITS 1] (the 10th-edition second Waaagh! is gone).
- **Da Big Hunt — Da Hunt is On**: Beast Snagga attacks against MONSTER / VEHICLE units have +1 AP (replaces the 10th-edition Prey rule).
- **Dread Mob — Try Dat Button!**: when an Ork WALKER is selected to shoot or fight, roll one or two D6 (free rule choice): +1 A / +2 S / +1 AP; two dice add a hazard roll after the unit attacks.
- **Shoota Boyz — Dakka! Dakka! Dakka!**: Ork INFANTRY ranged weapons have [ASSAULT], and +3" Range while riled up.
- **Flyboyz — Skyborne Loons**: Ork AIRCRAFT do not count towards the strategic-reserves points limit; riled-up AIRCRAFT / Deffkoptas get +1 to hit with ranged attacks.
- **Kult of Speed — Adrenaline Junkies**: Warbikers are BATTLELINE; Speed Freeks that advance or fall back gain [ASSAULT] and can still charge.
- **Blitz Brigade — Unstoppable Momentum**: WAGON advance rolls become a 6 and WAGON units get a free charge re-roll.
- **Green Tide — Mob-handed Brutality**: Boyz melee attacks have [SUSTAINED HITS 1]; Ork INFANTRY that charged get [LETHAL HITS] against non-MONSTER/VEHICLE targets.
- **Taktikal Brigade — Suspiciously Well Organised**: Stormboyz are BATTLELINE; Boyz / Kommandos / Stormboyz can charge after falling back (the 10th-edition Taktiks are gone).
- The AI answers every new choice (free re-roll, War Cry, Try Dat Button!, Hop Splat, Roar of Mork, Visions of Violence) without pausing the game.
