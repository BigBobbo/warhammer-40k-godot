# WP-M3b — Codex: Space Marines (11e), stratagems of eight generic detachments (player-facing bullets)

- All 24 stratagems of Terminator Storm Force, Assault Brethren, Gravis Linebreaker Force, Devastator Brethren, Gravis Siege Force, Tactical Brethren, Ironclad Champions and Tacticus Firestorm Force now work for every Space Marine army (generic or a chapter such as Space Wolves): each is offered in the stratagem pop-up at the moment its WHEN names, costs its printed CP and applies its printed effect. The AI uses or declines them on its own and never stalls the game.
- Defence: TACTICAL DREADNOUGHT FORTITUDE (-1 Damage) and ARMOUR OF CONTEMPT (-1 AP) when an enemy targets your unit in melee or in your opponent's Shooting phase, until that enemy has attacked; DUTY IN DEATH lets slain models fight on (4+, +1 with the Assault Doctrine).
- Attack buffs when your unit is selected to shoot or fight: MERCILESS VETERANS, ANNIHILATING FORCE, STORM OF FIRE (+1 AP with the Devastator Doctrine), GENE-WROUGHT MIGHT ([LANCE], +1 AP with the Assault Doctrine), POINT-BLANK BRUTALITY (engaged Tacticus units fire their rifles at point-blank range), and the two-option MERCY IS WEAKNESS / RELENTLESS ASSAULT (one button per option).
- After your unit has shot: PURGATION PUSH (move up to 5" onto an objective), SUPPRESSION VOLLEYS (battle-shock roll at -1) and DOMINATION FIRE (the enemy is suppressed: -1 to hit until your next turn) — pick the enemy unit hit in a follow-up pop-up. HAIL OF VENGEANCE shoots back at an enemy that killed one of your models.
- Movement, charges and objectives: STAND UNYIELDING secures an objective at the end of your Movement phase, MASTERFUL TACTICS moves your unit when an enemy ends a move within 8" (6" flat with the Tactical Doctrine), UPSTOPPABLE ADVANCE gives Dreadnoughts MOBILE, ARMOURED IMPACT deals mortal wounds after a Gravis charge, ADAMANTINE TERROR battle-shocks enemies engaged with a Dreadnought at the start of the Fight phase, GUNSHIP EXTRACTION pulls Terminators back into strategic reserves at the end of your opponent's Fight phase.
- FOR THE EMPEROR! (+1 OC) can be used in your Command phase AND in your opponent's — a new pop-up opens for you when your opponent ends their Command step.
- The game log names every stratagem used, the dice rolled for battle-shock tests and mortal wounds, and the option chosen.
