# WP-C46 — Codex: Adeptus Custodes detachments + enhancements (player-facing bullets)

- All 13 Codex: Adeptus Custodes (11th edition) detachment rules now play as printed:
  - **Guardians of the Throne — Martial Mastery:** when a Custodes unit is selected to fight, a free card asks [SUSTAINED HITS 1] or [LETHAL HITS] for its melee attacks this phase.
  - **Aquilan Shield — Gilded Guardians:** ranged attacks with Strength above the target's Toughness get -1 to wound against Custodes units (not Monsters/Vehicles) standing within range of an objective.
  - **Dread Host — Instruments of the Emperor's Wrath:** Custodes units can re-roll charge rolls; the free re-roll dialog is named after the rule.
  - **Emperor's Chosen — Magna Imperator:** a Custodes unit selected to attack re-rolls its first failed hit and its first failed wound.
  - **Emissaries Imperatus — Heralds of the Throne:** Custodes units have Fights First; the fight panel lists them in the Fights First step.
  - **Shadowkeepers — Wardens of the Dark Cells:** the melee version of Gilded Guardians.
  - **Solar Watch — Talon Sortie:** falling back no longer stops a Custodes unit from charging.
  - **Honoured Companions — Companion's Watch:** Trusted Sentinel units within range of an objective re-roll wound rolls of 1.
  - **Auric Champions — Assemblage of Might:** in your Command phase a free card lets you pick a dreadful foe; your Custodes Characters get +1 to wound against it until your next Command phase.
  - **Lions of the Emperor — On Gilded Wings:** at the end of your opponent's Fight phase a free card can lift an unengaged Terminator unit into strategic reserves.
  - **Might of the Moritoi — Moritoi Ancients:** undamaged Dreadnoughts have +2 OC; damaged ones re-roll hit rolls of 1, and below half strength they also re-roll wound rolls of 1.
  - **Grav-Assault Force — Flare Shields:** Grav-Assault units have a 4+ invulnerable save against shooting.
  - **Null Maiden Vigil — Silent Sisterhood:** an Anathema Psykana Character can be your Warlord, Prosecutor Squads are Battleline, and Creeping Dread makes nearby enemy Psykers and damaged units take a battle-shock test at -1 in your opponent's Command phase. The battle-shock card now shows the -1 ("-1 Creeping Dread", "= 8 (-1) = 7").
- Each Shield Host detachment's Favoured Ka'tah gives its extra effect (e.g. Solar Watch Calistus +2" Move, Dread Host Dacatarai -2" to enemy pile-ins and consolidations, Aquilan Shield Salvus +6" Range).
- All 29 codex enhancements are in the game, each doing what its card says. The ones you choose to use appear as free cards when they apply: Eagle's Eye (3+ invulnerable save when attacked, once per battle), Pareldor's Caducatrix (heal D3+3 at the start of a phase, once per battle), Inspirational Exemplar (lift a battle-shock at the start of a phase), Sally Forth (an advanced unit can still charge), Huntress' Eye (force a battle-shock test at -1 in your Movement phase), the Auriferous Orb's Blinding Light (-1 to hit for an enemy unit it hit). Unstoppable Destroyer adds a mode picker to the consolidation dialog and 4" pile-ins / consolidations. Emperor's Light and the Auriferous Orb add their weapons to the bearer; Eagle's Eye adds +1 Wound.
- Army building: each enhancement is checked against its printed bearer restriction, once per army and the per-1000-points cap; problems show as warnings when the army loads. The browser Army Builder now lists the 13 codex detachments (the old Shield Host / Talons / Silent Hunters / Solar Spearhead / Tharanatoi ones are hidden) and offers each Custodes character only the enhancements its detachment and restriction allow; armies saved from the builder keep their enhancements in the game.
- The old 10th-edition Custodes rules are gone: the Shield Host Martial Mastery choice at the start of the game, Against All Odds, the Talons auras, Silent Hunters, Admonimortis, Panoptispex, Auric Mantle and the 10e Castellan's Mark deployment step.
- AI: the computer picks Martial Mastery options, dreadful foes, On Gilded Wings and every enhancement choice by itself and never leaves a card waiting.
- Not yet playable by clicking: the Castellan's Mark redeploy (the rules engine offers up to three Custodes redeploys / strategic-reserves moves, but the Redeployment step has no player controls yet — the same gap as Razgit's Magik Map — and the AI skips redeploys). The browser Army Builder still uses its older Custodes datasheets for unit stats (e.g. Shield-Captain W6) — only its detachments and enhancements were updated.
