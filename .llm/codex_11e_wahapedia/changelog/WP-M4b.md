# WP-M4b — Codex: Space Marines generic datasheet abilities, part B (player-facing bullets)

- The datasheet and wargear abilities of 29 generic Space Marine datasheets (11th edition codex) now do what their cards say, for every Space Marine army (Space Marines and every chapter): Intercessor, Incursor, Scout, Heavy Intercessor, Assault Intercessor, Terminator, Eliminator, Hellblaster, Aggressor, Sternguard Veteran and Desolation Squads, Bladeguard Ancient, Ancient in Terminator Armour, Captain in Gravis / Phobos Armour, Chaplain, Lieutenant, Lieutenant in Phobos Armour, Librarian, Chief Librarian Tigurius, Adrax Agatone, Darnath Lysander, Tor Garadon, Storm Speeder Thunderstrike, Gladiator Reaper and Valiant, Land Raider Redeemer, Impulsor and Astraeus.
- Abilities you choose to use appear as free "Ability" cards in the stratagem window when they apply:
  - **Your Command phase:** **Strategic Acumen** (Captains in Gravis / Phobos Armour pick a combat doctrine for their unit), then **Demi-company Commander** (a Lieutenant takes the same doctrine).
  - **Start of any phase:** the Chaplain's **Spiritual Leader** (a battle-shocked unit within 6" recovers, once per battle round) and Darnath Lysander's **Rampart** (2+ invulnerable save for the phase, once per battle).
  - **Your Movement phase:** Tigurius' **Prescience** (+1 Sv until your next turn) and the Librarian's **Force Dome** (4+ invulnerable save until your next turn) and **Veil of Time** (when advancing: the advance roll is a 6).
  - **Your Shooting phase:** Tigurius' **Telepathic Assault** (2D3 mortal wounds within 24"), the Incursors' **Haywire Mine** (once per battle) and **Divinator-class Auspexes** (scan an enemy: re-roll hit rolls of 1 against it), Tor Garadon's **Signum Array** (an enemy loses the benefit of cover), the Eliminators' **Special-issue Optics** ([IGNORES COVER] or +6" detection range on an enemy).
  - **After shooting:** the Storm Speeder Thunderstrike's **Shattered Defences** (+1 AP against a monster or vehicle it hit), the Astraeus' **Suppression Fire** (-1 to hit for a unit its macro-accelerator hit), the Captain in Phobos Armour's **Tactical Fluidity** (move D6"; or fall back 6" at the end of your opponent's Fight phase).
  - **Start of the battle:** the Terminator Squad's **Teleport Homer** token (Rapid Ingress next to it is 1 CP cheaper).
- Tigurius (psyker level 3) and the Librarian (psyker level 1) follow the Librarius limit: each psychic power rolls a D6 (on a 1 the unit is battle-shocked).
- Passive abilities apply automatically and are named in the dice log: Bolter Discipline, Tactical Mainstay, Flexible Asset, Raise the Banner (secures objectives at the end of your Movement phase), Deeds of Legend, Refuse to Yield, Litany of Hate, Unyielding in the Face of the Foe, Fury of the First, Tactical Precision, Targeted Intercession, Thunderstrike, Hood of Hellfire, Psychic Hood, Reaping Tally, Chameleoline Cloaks, Priority Target Acquisition, Rites of Thermal Appeasement, Close-quarters Firestorm, Lord of the Pyroclasts, Unto the Anvil, Icon of Obstinacy, Assault Ramp (assault disembark after the Redeemer moves), Wrath of the Machine Spirit, Veteran Marksmen, Targeter Optics, Siege Captain, Rapid Disembarkation (disembark after the Impulsor advances), Never Shall the Standard Fall.
- Wargear: the Lieutenant's Storm Shield (4+ invulnerable save) and the Impulsor's Shield Dome (5+ invulnerable save) and Orbital Comms Array (Scouts 6") now work.
- Fix: these datasheets fire only the weapons they carry — e.g. Intercessors no longer get every Sergeant melee option, single characters and vehicles no longer fire every optional weapon when the list shows their default kit, and Scout / Desolation Squad weapon swaps land on the right models.
