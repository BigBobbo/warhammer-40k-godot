# WP-S4 — Codex: Space Marines (Space Wolves) datasheet abilities (player-facing bullets)

- Every datasheet and wargear ability on the 21 Space Wolves datasheets (11th edition codex) now does what its card says, for Space Wolves armies.
- Abilities you choose to use appear as free "Ability" cards in the stratagem window when they apply:
  - **Start of the first battle round:** Ulrik the Slayer's **Slayer's Oath** (pick an enemy CHARACTER/MONSTER/VEHICLE: attacks against it ignore hit modifiers; Oathbound melee +1 to hit, +1 to wound against it) and the Wolf Guard Headtakers' **Headhunters** (pick a quarry: [DEVASTATING WOUNDS] and [PRECISION] against it; pick again when it dies).
  - **Your Command phase:** the Wolf Priest's **Healing Balms** (heal D3+1 wounds; destroyed models can come back).
  - **Your Movement phase:** Logan Grimnar's **High King of Fenris** (a unit in strategic reserves arrives as if it were one battle round later — even in round 1), the Iron Priest's **Gift of the Iron Wolf** (a Space Wolves vehicle within 3" heals D3 and ignores hit/wound penalties until your next Movement phase) and Njal Stormcaller's **Murderous Hurricane** (an enemy within 12" cannot Fire Overwatch this turn).
  - **Your Shooting phase:** Njal's **Tempest's Wrath** (an enemy within 18" is suppressed: -1 to hit until your next turn) and the Wolf Scouts' **Haywire Mine** (once per battle: D3 mortal wounds to an enemy within 6", 2D3 against vehicles).
  - **Your opponent's turn:** **Predatory Instinct** (Fenrisian Wolves move D6" when an enemy ends a move within 8"), **Storm Caller** (Njal's unit gets -3" detection range when targeted), **Bestial Rage** (the Wulfen Dreadnought surges D6+1" after losing wounds to a shooting attack).
  - **After the Wulfen with Storm Shields fight:** **Hammer Blow** suppresses an enemy monster or vehicle they hit.
- Njal's three powers are psychic: each use rolls a D6 (on a 1 his unit is battle-shocked) and Librarius limits him to 3 psychic levels per battle round.
- Declare Battle Formations has a new **Unit splits** section: **Old Greymanes** splits Grey Hunters into two packs, **Let Loose the Wolves** splits Wolf Guard Headtakers into a Headtaker unit and a Hunting Wolves unit. Murderfang can no longer be chosen as Warlord.
- Passive abilities apply automatically and are named in the dice log: Battle-lust, War Howl (depends on whether Ragnar leads Blood Claws or Headtakers), Wind Walker, Guile of the Wolf (all three combat doctrines for Logan's unit), Legendary Tenacity, Ancient Tactician (-1 CP on Bjorn once per turn), Berserk Charge, Cunning Hunters, Judgement of the Omnissiah, the Iron Priest's Lone Operative near vehicles, Savage Frenzy, Death Totem, Murder-maker / Anvil of Endurance / Heroic Last Stand (models fight on after dying), Bestial Fury, Champion of the Kingsguard, Rugged Resilience, Thunderous Charge, Hunting Hounds (Fenrisian Wolves and Hunting Wolves count for objectives near a Space Wolves character), Fervour of the Ancients, Litany of Hate, Tempered Ferocity, Violent Fury, Deadly Stalkers.
- Storm Shields (+1 Wound or a 4+ invulnerable save) and Blizzard Shields (4+ invulnerable save) are applied to the models that took them.
- Fix: the "Feel No Pain X+" ability on datasheets (Ulrik, Bjorn, Murderfang, Wulfen Dreadnought, and also Trajann Valoris and the Beastboss) now actually works.
