# WP-M3a — Codex: Space Marines (11e), generic detachment stratagems A (player-facing bullets)

- 24 Space Marine stratagems now work for every Space Marine army (and any chapter army that takes these detachments): Gladius Task Force (6), Ironstorm Spearhead, Stormlance Task Force, Gauntlet Task Force, Phobos Shadow Force, Tacticus Attack Force and Phobos Shock Force (3 each). Each is offered in the stratagem pop-up at the moment its WHEN names, costs its printed CP and applies its printed effect; the AI uses or declines them on its own and never stalls the game.
- Command phase: ADAPTIVE STRATEGY (pick a combat doctrine for one unit — one button per unit and doctrine) and MIGHT OF THE MACHINE SPIRIT (a tank ignores penalties to its Move, BS, hit and wound rolls until your next Command phase) appear when you end the Command step; after using one, the pop-up returns for the other.
- Shooting / Fight buffs when your unit is selected to shoot or fight: STORM OF DEVASTATION ([IGNORES COVER]), HEADHUNTER DOCTRINE ([LETHAL HITS] vs Monsters and Vehicles), RELENTLESS ASSAULT (choose [SUSTAINED HITS 1] vs infantry or [LETHAL HITS] vs Monsters/Vehicles), STRIKE FROM THE SHADOWS (+1 Strength and [LETHAL HITS] for a hidden Phobos unit), MIGHT OF ANGELS ([LANCE]).
- Defensive reactions: ARMOUR OF CONTEMPT (-1 AP to attacks against your unit until that enemy has attacked), HURTLING TARGETS and UMBRAL EVASION (-1 to hit for ranged attacks against your Mounted/Speeder or Phobos unit), A WORTHY DEATH (destroyed models fight on, 4+) and LAYERED CERAMITE (Feel No Pain 5+ against mortal wounds for a Vehicle — from the S panel, use it before the mortal wounds land).
- Moves: RESPONSIVE TACTICS and TRANSHUMAN REACTIONS (move up to D6" — 6" with the tactical doctrine — when an enemy ends a move within 8" in their turn), SUDDEN ONSLAUGHT (advance roll becomes a 6), AGGRESSIVE DISEMBARKATION (disembarking models only need to be set up within 3" of the transport, not wholly within), WIND-SWIFT EVASION (at the end of the Fight phase your Mounted unit makes a normal move — or falls back if engaged — with the move panel: drag the unit or Auto-move, then Confirm), DUTY IS NEVER DONE (re-embark at the end of the Fight phase), TACTICAL WITHDRAWAL (back into strategic reserves), TACTICAL FOCUS (consolidate up to D3+3"), TRANSHUMAN SWIFTNESS (Fights First and fight next).
- Objectives: STORM AND SECURE secures an objective your transport controls; MORTIS SNARES booby-traps an objective your Phobos/Scout unit controls — the first enemy unit that ends a move there rolls a D6 and on 2+ suffers D6 mortal wounds.
- Fix: Space Marine units can now embark in Space Marine transports (Rhino, Impulsor, Repulsor, Land Raider, Drop Pod, …) — the transport's "SPACE MARINES INFANTRY" capacity text was never matched.
- The game log names every stratagem used, the doctrine / option chosen and every die rolled; the dice log's modifier notes name the stratagems on the rolls they changed.
