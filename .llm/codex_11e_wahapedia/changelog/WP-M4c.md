# WP-M4c — Codex: Space Marines datasheet abilities, M4c datasheets (player-facing bullets)

- The datasheet and wargear abilities of 29 Space Marine datasheets (11th edition codex) now work for every Space Marine army: Captain on Bike, Gladiator Lancer, Judiciar, Kayvaan Shrike, Kor'sarro Khan, Land Speeder, Outrider Squad, Redemptor Dreadnought, Reiver Squad, Repulsor, Repulsor Executioner, Rhino, Vulkan He'stan, Company Heroes, Apothecary, Ancient, Assault Intercessors with Jump Packs, Aethon Shaan, Caanok Var, Suboden Khan, Cato Sicarius, Victrix Honour Guard, Captain Titus, Wardens of Ultramar, both Eradicator Squads, Marneus Calgar, Invader ATVs and Kaius Konorius.
- Abilities you choose appear as free "Ability" cards in the stratagem window:
  - **Your Command phase:** Captain on Bike's **Strategic Acumen** (pick a combat doctrine for his unit), the Apothecary's **Narthecium** (heal D3+1), Aethon Shaan's **Master of Shadows** (an infantry unit re-rolls charges).
  - **Your Movement phase:** Marneus Calgar's **Master Tactician** (a doctrine for a unit within 9"); Vulkan He'stan's **Seeker of the Unfound** (pick his objective: OC 10, Ld 5+, Feel No Pain 4+ near it).
  - **Your Shooting phase:** Vulkan's **Forgefather** (+2 S for melta/torrent attacks against one enemy), the Heavy Bolter Eradicators' **Overlapping Destruction** ([BLAST 1] against one enemy), Caanok Var's **Cold and Calculating**, the Land Speeder's **Purgation Run** (move D6" after shooting).
  - **Fight phase:** Captain Titus's **Righteous Fury** (once per battle: +1 S, he heals when his unit has fought), the Victrix Honour Guard's **Banner of Macragge** (once per battle: +1 A and S), Caanok Var's **Cold and Calculating**.
  - **Charges and reactions:** Assault Intercessors with Jump Packs' **Hammer of Wrath** (mortal wounds when they end a charge), the Repulsor's **Combat Embarkation** (a charged unit next to it jumps in), Cato Sicarius's **Knight Champion of Macragge** (moves 6" when an enemy ends a move near him), the Victrix's **Glory of Ultramar** (surge after losing a model), Kayvaan Shrike's **Echo of the Ravenspire** (back to strategic reserves at the end of the opponent's Fight phase).
- Declare Battle Formations has a new Codex: Space Marines section: **Thunderhawk Insertion** (Calgar gives a Gravis/Phobos/Tacticus unit Deep Strike) and **Cerebrex Logic Engine** (Caanok Var gives an infantry unit Scouts 6").
- Passive abilities apply automatically: Into the Fray, Aquilon Optics, Tempormortis, Trifold Path of Shadow, Trophy Taker, For the Khan!, Full-throttle Assault, Duty Eternal, Terror Troops, Fearsome Assault, Grapnel Launchers, Grav-chutes, Executioner, Veiling Smoke and Blackwing Mantle (-1 CP stratagems), Raise the Banner, Command Squad, Honour of the Company, Spear of Chogoris, Skilled Riders, Captain of the Honour Guard, Honour or Death, Honour Guard of Macragge, Press the Attack, Honour of Ultramar, Codex Adept, Aggressive Reconnaissance, Veteran Bodyguard, Calgar's Champion, Total Obliteration.
- Fix: Space Marine units can embark in Space Marine transports again (Rhino, Repulsor, Land Raider, Impulsor, Drop Pod and the rest).
- Fix: Space Marine characters and vehicles no longer fire every optional weapon on their datasheet; Company Heroes and Wardens of Ultramar models each carry their own weapons.
