# WP-M4a — Codex: Space Marines (11e), generic datasheet abilities (list A)

- 29 generic Space Marine datasheets now play their codex abilities in every Space Marine army (any Chapter): Captains, Lieutenant with Combi-weapon, the Librarians, the Chaplains, Iron Father Feirros, Techmarine, Roboute Guilliman, Bladeguard, Terminator Assault, Vanguard Veterans, Inceptors, Infernus, Infiltrators, Land Raiders, Drop Pod, Thunderhawk, Storm Speeders, Ballistus / Brutalis Dreadnoughts, Invictor Warsuit, Firestrike Servo-turrets, Apothecary Biologis.
- New free "Ability" cards (no CP) in the stratagem dialog: Strategic Acumen (pick a doctrine for the Captain's unit), Priority Target Identified (pick a terrain feature), Helix Gauntlet heal, Teleport Homer token and Forward Assault Warsuit mark at the start of the battle, Author of the Codex, Blessings of the Omnissiah / Master of the Forge, Assault Ramp / Aerial Assault (disembark after the transport moved), Thunderhawk Cluster Bombs, Thunderous Force / Soul Sight / Might of Heroes / Shrouding (psychic, Librarius budget), Omni-scramblers, Catechism of Fire, Hailstrike / Driven from Cover, Bladeguard, Finest Hour, Evade and Survive, Parabolic Jetleap. The AI uses the combat ones.
- Passive rules: Vivispectral Analysis Targeting, Fury of the Machine Spirit (Lethal Hits), Ballistus Strike / Hammerstrike / Terminatus Assault (Sustained Hits), Litany of Hate, Exhortation of Rage, Vanguard Assault, Meteoric Descent, Primarch of the XIII aura, Codex Adept, Power of the Machine Spirit (re-roll one hit and one wound), Rites of Tempering, Psychic Hood / Zealous Fortitude (Feel No Pain 4+), Angel's Wrath, Unstoppable Valour, the conditional Lone Operative of Iron Father / Techmarine / Guilliman.
- Sentinel Protocols: Firestrike Servo-turrets can Fire Overwatch even after another unit did this phase (the window is offered again) and hit on 4+. Brutalis Charge: Crushing Impact costs 1CP less and ignores its once-per-phase limit.
- Drop Pods start in strategic reserves automatically, can arrive in the first battle round, and the units inside disembark at once (more than 8" from the enemy); an emptied Drop Pod cannot be re-embarked.
- Wargear: Relic Shield and the Terminator Assault Squad's Storm Shields give +1 W, Combat Shields a 5+ invulnerable save.
- Fixes: Space Marine single-model datasheets with no wargear listed (Land Raider, Captains, Brutalis, Thunderhawk, Invictor ...) no longer fire every weapon option — they use their printed loadout; taking a Relic Shield now removes the weapon it replaces; Land Raiders, the Drop Pod and the Thunderhawk can be embarked again (their transport keywords were misread); the old 10th-edition Omni-scramblers 12" deep-strike block no longer applies to 11th-edition Space Marine armies.
