# WP-G2 — Codex reserves / transport abilities (player-facing bullets)

- Assault Vehicle (Coronus Grav-Carrier, Anathema Psykana Rhino): after the transport ends an Advance move in your Movement phase, the units inside can still disembark — a "shock disembark" (set up within 3"; the unit can't declare a charge this turn). The Disembark dialog says "ASSAULT VEHICLE — Shock Disembark" instead of "Cannot disembark". (The Impulsor's Rapid Disembarkation now shows the same dialog.)
- Pilin' Out (Trukk): in your opponent's Movement phase, when an enemy unit ends a move within 8" of your Trukk, a free "Pilin' Out" card pops up; using it lets the units inside disembark (set up within 3"; they can't declare a charge this turn). Each unit can also stay embarked. The computer opponent uses it too.
- Deff from the Shadows (Boss Snikrot): at the end of your opponent's Movement phase, if Snikrot is in strategic reserves, the end-of-phase pop-up offers him a free ingress move ("Arrive (free)") — from the first battle round. The usual ingress set-up rules apply (wholly within 6" of a battlefield edge, more than 8" from enemies, not in the enemy deployment zone before round 3).
- Swift Ruin (Venatari with Verutum Lances): the Rapid Ingress pop-up shows each unit's own cost — the Venatari read "Arrive (0 CP)" with a Swift Ruin note — and after a unit arrives the pop-up is offered again, so the Venatari AND another unit can arrive with Rapid Ingress in the same phase.
- The Rapid Ingress pop-up only lists units that can actually be targeted right now, each with its cost.
