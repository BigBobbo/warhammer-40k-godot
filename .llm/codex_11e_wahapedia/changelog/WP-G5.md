# WP-G5 — player-controlled Custodes reactive stratagems (player-facing bullets)

- NO ESCAPE (Shadowkeepers) is now yours to play: after you pay the CP a NO ESCAPE window lets you click Roll Charge, pick the charge target(s) — only enemy units that fell back this phase and are within your roll are listed — and click Charge; you then drag your models into engagement range on the board (the unit's panel reads "Charge — NO ESCAPE (up to N")") and click End This Unit's Move. The move follows the normal charge rules (distance, engaged with every target and no other enemy unit, coherency, base contact where possible); "Charge fails (don't move)" or ending the move without moving lets the charge fail.
- SELFLESS SERVICE (Emissaries Imperatus) now follows its printed text: you roll the charge yourself (a result above 6 is changed to 6) and may pick ANY enemy unit(s) within 6" and within the roll — not just the closest one — then make the move with the normal charge drag tools (Snap to Contact / Confirm Charge Moves).
- RAPID REACTIONS (Aquilan Shield) now lets you choose which weapons fire (and which profile) at the enemy unit that fell back, then click Roll Attacks; a results window shows the hit, wound and save rolls and the models slain.
- The computer opponent still uses all three on its own and never stalls the game; in online games they keep the previous automatic resolution.
