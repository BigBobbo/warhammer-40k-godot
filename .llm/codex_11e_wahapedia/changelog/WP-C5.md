# WP-C5 — Codex: Adeptus Custodes stratagems (player-facing bullets)

- All 41 Codex: Adeptus Custodes stratagems of the 13 detachments now work: each is offered in the stratagem pop-up at the exact moment its WHEN names (or from the S panel when it has no single trigger), costs its printed CP and applies its printed effect. The AI uses or declines them on its own and never stalls the game.
- New moments the pop-up can appear at: when a unit ends an Advance move, when a unit is picked to arrive from reserves (before you place it), when a unit is picked to pile in or consolidate (before the move), when an enemy unit has fought, at the start and the end of your opponent's Charge phase, and at the end of any phase.
- Fight: ANATHEMA BLADEMASTERY ([SUSTAINED HITS 1] or [LETHAL HITS]), GRIM RESPONSIBILITY ([LETHAL HITS] vs Characters / Monsters), AVENGE THE FALLEN (+2 A), BEARERS OF HIS LIGHT (ignore hit / wound penalties), SLAYERS OF NIGHTMARES (+1 to wound vs tougher units), DUTY UNTO DEATH (destroyed models fight on a 3+), SHIELD OF HONOUR (-1 to hit / wound for enemies that ignore your unit), INDOMITABLE GUARDIANS (fight next with Fights First), LIGHTNING WRATH (D3+3" pile-in), EMPEROR'S DOMAIN (objective consolidation while engaged), HONOURED INTERRED (re-roll 1s to hit near a Dreadnought).
- Shooting: PSY-CHAFF VOLLEY (+1 AP vs the prosecuted unit — you pick it from the units you hit), PURGATION SWEEP (+1/+2 A for torrent weapons), PRIME TARGET (a free hit, wound and damage re-roll — click the die in the dice panel), TIP OF THE TALON (+1 S within 9"), ADVANCED STABILISERS (shoot after advancing), INEVITABLE ANNIHILATION (ignore BS / hit penalties, e.g. cover), VIGIL UNENDING (-1 Damage) and IN AURAMITE CLAD (-1 AP) when the enemy targets you.
- Movement: UNSTOPPABLE MOMENTUM, UNCEASING ONSLAUGHT, UNLIMITED ENDURANCE, PRETERNATURAL RAPIDITY, AT SPEAR'S LENGTH, MANOEUVRE AND FIRE, INEXORABLE, IMPENETRABLE BASTION (secure an objective), GOLDEN LIGHT OF THE MOIRAIDES (arrive from reserves with -1 to be hit and no overwatch), and the reactions in your opponent's Movement phase: SWIFT AS THE EAGLE (D3+3" move), NO ESCAPE (charge a unit that fell back), RAPID REACTIONS (shoot a unit that fell back).
- Charge / other: FURY OF THE EMPEROR (+2 to charge after arriving), GRAVIMETRIC GRENADE (-1 to an enemy's charge rolls), SELFLESS SERVICE (charge at the end of your opponent's Charge phase, path tools labelled SELFLESS SERVICE), SUPERHUMAN FOCUS (ready a unit at the end of a phase), VICTORY BEFORE DEATH (secure an objective when a grav-tank dies), UNLEASH THE LIONS (split a Terminator unit into single models), GILDED CHAMPION (use a once-per-battle ability such as Moment Shackle again).
- The game log names every stratagem used (and the option chosen); secondary picks (which enemy unit, which objective) open a small picker after you click Use.
- Fix: Trajann Valoris' Moment Shackle choice now pops up at the start of the Fight phase for a human player (it was offered by the rules but the pop-up never appeared, leaving the Fight phase waiting).
