# WP-S3 — Codex: Space Marines (11e), Space Wolves stratagems (player-facing bullets)

- All 12 Space Wolves detachment stratagems now work (Champions of Fenris, Saga of the Beastslayer, Saga of the Great Wolf): each is offered in the stratagem pop-up at the moment its WHEN names, costs its printed CP and applies its printed effect. The AI uses or declines them on its own and never stalls the game.
- Command phase: BIRTH OF A SAGA (the unit becomes a CHARACTER until your next Command phase) and GRIMNAR'S COMMAND (pick a combat doctrine for one unit — one button per unit and doctrine) appear when you end the Command step; after using one, the pop-up returns so you can still use the other.
- Fight: CHAMPION'S GUIDANCE (re-roll 1s to hit and wound), UNBRIDLED FEROCITY ([LANCE]), FANGS OF THE PACK ([PRECISION]) and EYE OF THE PACK (re-roll wound rolls of 1 and 2 — also in your Shooting phase) when your unit is selected to fight / shoot; HEROIC RESOLVE (-1 Damage to attacks against your Character until that enemy unit has attacked) when an enemy targets your Character in melee or in your opponent's Shooting phase.
- Movement reactions: IMPETUOSITY (surge D6" toward the enemy) and BATTLE INSTINCTS (move D3+3") after an enemy unit that targeted your unit has shot — the move panel lets you drag the unit or Auto-move it; FENRISIAN FEROCITY (MOBILE: move through dense terrain) when your Mounted / Walker unit moves or declares a charge; CO-ORDINATED STRIKE (back into strategic reserves) at the end of your opponent's Fight phase.
- WOLF TOTEMS (Feel No Pain 5+ against mortal wounds) is used from the S panel in any phase — use it before the mortal wounds land (for example before firing supercharged plasma); it lasts until the end of that phase.
- The game log names every stratagem used and the doctrine chosen; the dice log's modifier notes name Champion's Guidance, Eye of the Pack and [LANCE] on the rolls they changed.
