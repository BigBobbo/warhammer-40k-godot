# WP-S1 — Codex: Space Marines (11e), Space Wolves data

- Space Wolves datasheets use Codex: Space Marines profiles, points, weapons and wargear options (e.g. Logan Grimnar T6 for 150 pts, Grey Hunters T5 for 180 pts, Wolf Guard Battle Leader 80 pts).
- Space Wolves armies get the codex army rule Combat Doctrines on every unit; the index Oath of Moment no longer appears on them.
- The three Space Wolves codex detachments (Champions of Fenris, Saga of the Beastslayer, Saga of the Great Wolf) carry their codex Detachment Points, rules, enhancements and stratagems; Saga of the Hunter, Saga of the Bold, Legends of Saga and Song and Veterans of the Fang are retired.
- Wargear options now resolve per model in the engine: a Grey Hunter Pack Leader with a power fist AND a plasma pistol, a Venerable Dreadnought whose Fenrisian great axe replaces its assault cannon and combat weapon.
