# WP-M2a — Codex: Space Marines (11e), army rules for every Space Marine army + 7 detachments

- Combat Doctrines now works for every Space Marine army (Space Marines and every chapter), not only Space Wolves: pick Assault, Devastator or Tactical from the COMBAT DOCTRINES block of the Command panel at the start of your Command phase; each once per battle, it lasts until your next Command phase, and the Game Log says what is active. The AI picks one too. The old 10th-edition Gladius-only doctrine prompt is gone in 11th edition games.
- Transhuman Strategist: a Captain (or any model with the ability) as your Warlord gains you 1CP at the start of each battle round.
- Librarius: psykers have a psyker level per battle round (Librarians 1, Chief Librarian Tigurius 3); their psychic abilities spend it (the abilities themselves arrive with the datasheet work).
- Gladius Task Force — Codex Discipline: one doctrine can be picked a second time in the battle (the button says so). Enhancements: Standard of the Emperor Ascendant (+1 OC, better Leadership, Feel No Pain 5+, and a once-per-battle ANCIENT EXHORTATION card when its unit is selected to fight: +1 attack in melee), Laurels of Triumph (+1 S and AP in melee, +2 with the Assault doctrine), Adept of the Codex (the Captain's unit always has the Tactical doctrine too), Artificer Armour (2+ save, Feel No Pain 5+).
- Ironstorm Spearhead — vehicles (not walkers) re-roll one hit roll and one wound roll when they shoot each phase (shown in the dice log). Redoubtable Machine Spirit (5+ invulnerable save, heals 1 wound at the end of your Command phase), Gunnery Honours (the vehicle's guns count as [HEAVY]).
- Stormlance Task Force — bikes and speeders get +2" Move. Supercharged Engines gives a free advance re-roll; Auspex Triangulation Shrines offers a card at the start of your Shooting phase to spot an enemy within 12" (+3" detection range).
- Gauntlet Task Force — Combined Deployment: after a transport shoots, a free card marks one enemy unit it hit as assailed; units that disembarked this turn get [SUSTAINED HITS 1] against it. Linebreaker Onslaught (after disembarking: charge re-roll, no overwatch against the unit), Damocles-class Uplink (a Captain riding in a transport gives a nearby infantry unit the doctrine of your choice at the start of your Movement phase).
- Phobos Shadow Force — Shadow Masters: after a Phobos or Scout Squad unit shoots, a free card lets it stay hidden or make a D6" move (no charge after). Execute and Redeploy (a 6" move after shooting), Venator Omni-auspex (re-roll wound rolls of 1 against hidden units, 1-2 with the Assault doctrine).
- Tacticus Attack Force — Tacticus units get a free advance re-roll. Martial Paragon (re-roll one hit and one wound roll per phase), Spearpoint War Leader (Scouts 6").
- Phobos Shock Force — Vanguard Ambushers: hidden Phobos units at the end of your Movement phase re-roll wound rolls of 1 for the rest of the turn (Game Log). Seal of Shrouding: enemies cannot fire overwatch at the unit.
