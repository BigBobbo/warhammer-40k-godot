# WP-G3 — Codex: Orks (11e) movement gaps (player-facing bullets)

- Wazdakka Gutsmek's PULSE JET now works. Pick "Pulse Jet" with Full Throttle in your Command phase; in your Movement phase, select Wazdakka and press the new "Pulse Jet (18" + D6)" button. It rolls a D6 and Wazdakka can move up to 18" plus the roll, flying over every model and every piece of terrain (no vertical distance). It must end out of engagement range. For the rest of the turn it cannot declare a charge or start an action. Until the start of your next turn, every attack that targets it has -1 to hit, and the dice log's modifier notes name "Pulse Jet". The AI uses it too: far from the enemy it picks the Pulse Jet engine and jets toward its objective.
- Painboss — RUNNIN' WIV DA HERD: while the Painboss is attached to a Mounted unit (e.g. Squighog Boyz), it loses INFANTRY and gains MOBILE and MOUNTED (its datasheet card shows this). Every rule that checks keywords sees the change, for example anti-INFANTRY weapons and terrain movement. When the Painboss leaves the unit or its bodyguard is destroyed, its own keywords come back.
- Nobz — GET OUT DA WAY!: in a charge, the Nobz can now move through enemy models, except MONSTER and VEHICLE models. "Snap to Contact" routes them through an enemy screen to the target behind it.
- Charge moves now follow the 11e movement rule: a charging model cannot pass through enemy models. It can still pass through friendly models, AIRCRAFT never block, and FLY units charge over models. Dragging a model through an enemy base is refused, Snap to Contact goes around, and the AI plans its charges the same way.
