## Army rules

### Martial Ka’tah
At the start of your Command phase, friendly Adeptus Custodes units with this ability are readied . While a friendly unit is readied , you can select one of the ka’tah abilities presented below for that unit to use. If you do, that unit is no longer readied . Each ka’tah ability will tell you when that ka’tah ability can be used, and the effects that ka’tah ability has on the unit using it. We recommend placing a token next to each readied unit, removing it when that unit is no longer readied . Favoured Ka’tah Many of the most prominent shield hosts favour a specific martial ka’tah above all others. All detachments tagged ‘Unique: Shield Host’ state a Favoured Ka’tah. If you selected one of these detachments , the ka’tah ability that is the Favoured Ka’tah of that detachment grants the additional effect listed in that detachment . Example: If you selected the Solar Watch detachment , when you select the Calistus ka’tah ability for a readied unit, that unit has +2" M in addition to the normal effect of Calistus.

### Daughters of the Abyss
Friendly Anathema Psykana units with this ability have Feel No Pain 3+ against psychic attacks . Friendly ANATHEMA PSYKANA units with this ability have the following ability: Psychic Null (Aura): Friendly Adeptus Custodes units within 6" of this unit have Feel No Pain 5+ against psychic attacks .

### Anathema Psykana
You can include Anathema Psykana units in your army, even though they do not have the Adeptus Custodes faction keyword. When mustering your army, unless otherwise stated: You cannot select ANATHEMA PSYKANA as your army faction . You cannot select an ANATHEMA PSYKANA model to be your WARLORD .
