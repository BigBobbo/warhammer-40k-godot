# charge_r5 design brief: cluster `charge_vp_exchange`

**Verdict: no separator.** The stats, game and code analyses do not support any decision-time rule that separates the 7 KEY_clear charge positions (the AI charged; not charging was clearly better) from the 4 KEY_harmful ones (charging was clearly better). `cluster.json` therefore carries `"verdict": "no_separator"`. A design in this round may only fix a mechanism that has its own evidence. It must leave the guardrails unchanged, and it must not be presented as "the" fix for the cluster.

**Name.** The key is `charge_vp_exchange`, not `charge_exposure`. The measured mechanism is which side's mission pays for what the charge causes. Exposure to a counter-charge does not separate the classes: the enemy Heroically Intervened against the charging Stormboyz at both twins (s7469 clear, s7470_1 harmful; 3/3 made seeds each).

**Inputs.** The stats, game and code analyses of this step, the r4 panel (`tools/ai_lab/r4_designs/charge_low_odds/panel.json`), `features.json`, and five read-only merge checks (section 3). Line numbers refer to `40k/scripts/AIDecisionMaker.gd` as checked out (the shipped V10). Scale: 40 px per inch (`Measurement.PX_PER_INCH`, autoloads/Measurement.gd:7; `PIXELS_PER_INCH`, AIDecisionMaker.gd:31). The feature table's distances match the AI's printed distances within 0.06". Nothing tracked in the repo was changed. I made no new Godot runs for this brief; the checks use the game lens's 88 logs and the decoded saves.

## 2. What each lens found

**Stats** (features.json, 122 features; the 52 neutral charge-vs-no-charge rows average E −0.31, weighted −0.21 ± 0.19):
- No single feature separates the KEY set. The 14 perfect two-feature rules all hang on `vp_decider < 19.5` or `vp_diff > 13.5`, with gaps of at most 0.05 SD. Random labellings are easier to separate (mean 80 perfect pairs). A search-picked rule predicts a held-out KEY position 2/11; "always do not charge" gets 7/11.
- Best hypothesis, set in advance from the r4 twin contrast (**H1**): round 2 AND at least 1 other enemy Attached unit within 12" of the target. It gets 8/11 KEY; its 3 misses are the second charges s7419_2, s7470_2 and s7441_2, so it gets 7/7 on the rest; refit LOO 8/11. On the neutral set it flips 30 rows (18+/12−), mean +0.50 ± 0.39, weighted +0.27 ± 0.28, and it was picked among several variants (melee-focused enemy instead of Attached: similar; at most 3 friendly units within 3 inches: 35 flips, +0.22 ± 0.36). Not established.
- Low odds (p < 0.3) and the phantom overwatch estimate have no support in any set.
- Round 3: 16 of 19 round-3 positions favour charging (13/14 neutral plus all 3 round-3 harmful; the exceptions are s7474_r3_p1_1 and the second charges s7419_2 and s7441_2). p 0.0026 alone, 0.38 after the search correction; r4 saw the same direction.
- The best rules over all 63 measured rows (+29 to +39 VP) do not beat shuffled E (p 0.55–0.66).

**Game** (44 A/B pairs, seeds 5001–5004, replays and logs):
- The 4-seed mean B−A has the lab's sign at all 11 positions. W1 B−A is above 0 at 7/7 clear positions (mean +3.9) and below 0 at 3/4 harmful ones (mean −4.1; s7466, +0.5, pays later).
- The sign follows the mission. Clear: the opponent's mission pays for the charger's death or for the objective it gives up (s7469, s7457, s7419, s7467), or the decider's own primary loses an area (s7415). Harmful: the decider's mission pays (s7431 kill cards, s7403 Engage on All Fronts, s7466 Destroyer's Wrath), or the charger's death pays the opponent nothing (s7470_1).

**Code** (seed-5001 decision records, traced paths):
- The driver differs by position: gang-up from an earlier declaration (s7419_2, s7470_2; FIX_CHARGE_EV=1, OFF at line 1641, would remove both — traced, not run); an attached leader priced as a lone 7-W model (s7469, s7470_1, s7441); the post-roll re-pick at score 0.0 (s7457); "do not charge" valued only as the bar (~17196), which the chosen charges clear by 1.6× to 62.6×; FURY OF THE EMPEROR at a constant ai_score 2.5 over a 2.0 bar (CustodesCodexOther `ai_score`; AIDecisionMaker ~25192), so it is bought on every eligible Custodes declaration and priced nowhere in the charge score.
- The phantom overwatch multiplier (T7-51, 17085) fires on 4 of 11 chosen candidates and flips none. Removing it raises all three clear scores where it fires (s7415 8.08→8.66, s7419 1.72→5.69, s7467 1.60→6.68) and lifts harmful s7466 from 1.6× to 5.3× the bar.
- Decision-time context (friends, enemies near the target, objectives, VP, CP, Attached groups) is in the AI snapshot. Earlier charges in the same phase are not (the snapshot drops phase_log).

## 3. Merge checks (read-only; scripts in `.ai_lab_work/charge_r5/brief_work/`)

1. **11e charge rules** (wahapedia 11e core rules, fetched twice). 11.02 Charge Step: "If it is possible to make a charge move, and if you still want to, make a charge move with that unit (see below). Otherwise, your unit does not make a charge move." 11.04 Charge Move, Before Moving: "Select one or more enemy units that are within 12" of your unit and within the maximum distance of your unit; …" So targets are chosen after the roll. The engine follows 11.04 (ChargePhase ~906–925). The AI never uses the option to decline: `_select_post_roll_charge_targets` (18121) returns the best selectable target whatever its score. Logs: s7457 A, seeds 5001/5003/5004, "re-targeting Blade Champion Alpha (best of 1 selectable, score 0.0)"; s7403 A 5003 re-targeted CG Beta at 2.9 (bar 0.54); s7419 A 5003, the opponent's Scout Squad Beta re-targeted the Allarus at 0.0. The engine accepts SKIP_CHARGE after a roll (`_validate_skip_charge`, ChargePhase:464, checks only `completed_charges`), and the AI already sends it when its move planner finds no path (s7470_2 5001 A: "Burna Boyz Alpha cannot complete the charge move — charge fails").
2. **Overwatch.** All 88 game-lens logs carry "[11e 15.08] no Fire Overwatch against charging …" (ChargePhase:611). All 30 FIRE OVERWATCH uses in them happen in a Movement phase, none in a Charge phase.
3. **Heroic Intervention at the twins** (branch A). s7469: CG Alpha + BC Beta counter-charged the Stormboyz in 3/3 made seeds (1 success). s7470_1: CG Gamma counter-charged them in 3/3 made seeds (3 successes) and the Burna Boyz in the fourth. In the engine, leap_to_defend needs an enemy that charged within 12", and into_the_fray reaches any enemy within 6" with the roll capped at 6 (ChargePhase ~3846). At s7457 B, BC Alpha used into_the_fray on the Prosecutors in 4/4 seeds.
4. **Mission state at the decision** (decoded saves, `cards_at_decision.py`). The AI reads only its own active cards (every `get_active_missions` call passes `player`). Twins: at s7469 the opponent holds Overwhelming Force + Beacon (the target stands on nml_1); at s7470 it holds Assassination + A Tempting Target (Stormboyz are not characters). Harmful: at s7431 the decider holds Assassination + OF; at s7403 it holds Engage on All Fronts. A pre-specified card-only proxy — do not charge if the opponent holds a kill card the charger meets (OF near an objective, Assassination for a CHARACTER, Bring It Down for 10+ W models, No Prisoners, A Grievous Blow for 13+ models) and the decider holds none the target meets — gets 6/11 KEY (both twins and 4/4 harmful, but only s7419_2 and s7469 of the clear ones). It flips 4 of the 52 neutral rows (2+/2−, mean −0.47 ± 1.17; kept 48 rows, −0.30 ± 0.31). Output: `card_rule_check.json`.
5. **Hidden information and bench fixtures.** Rollouts replay the saved secondary deck order: at s7457, P1 drew Outflank + Overwhelming Force at its next Command phase in all 8 games (4 seeds × A/B). So E at card-driven positions is conditional on draws the AI cannot see. The three bench postdeploy saves (asym_2000, mirror_custodes_2000, mirror_orks_2000) have 0 attached units and a 16-card secondary deck per player (the mode field is empty; confirm tactical).

## 4. Agreement and conflict

- All three lenses agree that there is no single separator. The code lens finds a different driver at almost every position.
- Round 3 favours charging (stats 16/19; r4 5/5; r4 game reviewers).
- The second-charge positions are artefacts for the earlier charger (stats, game and code).
- Open conflict: the game lens says the VP exchange separates the classes, but the stats lens had no mission features, and the card-only proxy (check 4) does not generalise. A fuller VP-exchange model (cards + primaries + where the charger will stand) has not been tested on the neutral set. H1 and the card proxy both separate the twins; neither holds on the neutral rows.

## 6. Pitfalls

1. **Phantom 11e overwatch.** At edition ≥ 11 ChargePhase resolves no Fire Overwatch against a declared charge (check 2), yet `_estimate_overwatch_risk` (17791) multiplies every charge score (T7-51, 17085). Never use it as a cost. Removing it makes charges more likely, so bench that change on its own.
2. **Post-roll re-targeting leak.** Under 11.04 targets are picked after the roll, and the re-pick uses the unvetoed `_score_charge_target`, so any declaration-time veto leaks (r4). Use 11.02 instead: decline at the move (SKIP_CHARGE), for every member id of an Attached unit (`_attached_unit_target_id`), applied after `_apply_difficulty_noise` and after both Deadly Demise multipliers (17088 and 17766).
3. **Stompa / TITANIC.** `_estimate_overwatch_risk` has no edition, TITANIC or timing check. On the bench Orks lists the enemy Stompa (TITANIC; it can never Fire Overwatch) sets the estimate (4.4–11.3 dmg, r4 reviewers), so an overwatch-keyed rule measures Stompa proximity. Points-per-wound tests are skewed by UNLEASH THE LIONS (each split Allarus keeps 275 pts) and by the 215/225-pt Guard price gap.
4. **s7469 vs s7470_1** (section 5).
5. **Second charges** (s7415_2, s7419_2, s7470_2, s7441_2). The save lets the earlier charger declare again (`has_charged` is read at 17673 but never written; `_charge_coordination` is static and not saved). Do not fit a design to them. A change to the gang-up bookkeeping also changes s7470_1's branch A.
6. **Hidden information.** Rollouts replay the saved deck order: never read it; use draw odds. Confirm that 11e treats active secondary cards as open information before the AI reads the opponent's cards.
7. **Round 3 and later.** Limit any change that makes the AI charge less to battle rounds 1–2.
8. **Bench reach.** The bench fixtures have no Attached units and no reserves (r4; check 5), so Attached-keyed rules do not fire there. Report fires per fixture with their trigger.
9. **Engine fidelity.** Do not narrow `_weapon_carrier_count`: the engine lets every eligible Stormboy swing the Kustom Choppa (r4 root was disqualified for this). Fold Attached units per 19.02 instead.

## 7. Design angles (the same text is in `cluster.json` → `angles`)

**narrow.** Use the 11e option to decline the charge move. Core rules 11.02 make the move optional after a sufficient roll ('if you still want to') and 11.04 picks targets after the roll; _select_post_roll_charge_targets (AIDecisionMaker 18121) ignores the option and returns the best reachable target even at score 0.0. Behind a new param, when no declared target survives the roll and the best reachable target scores at or below the phase's charge threshold (computed at 17146-17186), return no targets and send SKIP_CHARGE (the engine accepts it after a roll; the AI already sends it when its move planner finds no path), with a [CHARGE-DECLINE] log line; the same hook is where any per-target veto must be honoured, for every member id of an Attached unit. Gate it to battle rounds 1-2. Expected KEY reach: s7457 only (re-pick of Blade Champion Alpha at score 0.0 in 3 of 4 replay seeds); s7403's seed-5003 re-pick scored 2.9 > 0.54 and must still move. The gain at s7457 may be partial: in B the Champion heroically intervenes into the Prosecutors anyway, and CG Delta' also died in the seed that reached the declared target. Measure s7457 over 36 seeds and count declines per bench fixture. Optional second arm with no new code: FIX_CHARGE_EV=1 at s7419_2, s7470_2 and s7470_1 (gang-up from an earlier, failed declaration decides s7419_2 and s7470_2 - traced, not run), reported separately.

**root.** Give 'not charging' a value and decide at the move, where 11e puts the commitment. Today 'do not charge' is only the bar (about 0.19-0.58 at Competitive, 17196) and the chosen charges clear it by 1.6x to 62.6x, so no bar retune separates the classes. At the 11.04 move step (and as an expected value over the 2D6 roll at declaration) compare the best reachable target's charge value with an explicit stay value for the unit: the OC it adds to an objective it scores or contests (s7467: the Allarus leave nml_2 and the Orks take it), own-primary and own-card areas it would leave (s7415: Death Trap terrain areas; CH-LEAVE x0.5 barely dents a 27.7x margin) and its next Shooting phase; price the CP a charge spends (FURY OF THE EMPEROR has a constant ai_score 2.5 over a 2.0 bar; Command Re-roll). Fold attached leaders into their Attached unit (19.02: 22 W, fight-back from every member - the r4 CLO-ATTACHED graft) and remove phantom overwatch at edition >= 11, each as its own separately benched sub-param. Do not expect root to separate the twins: folding moves s7469 and s7470_1 together and the stay value is low at both; show by replay that s7470_1 still charges (4 CP, BC Gamma) and that s7431 r3, s7403 r3 and s7466 still charge. Targets: s7415, s7467, and the leader-as-lone-model over-valuation at s7469 and s7441.

**game.** Price the VP exchange the charge sets up, from public mission state. The game lens found that the first-window VP exchange has the lab's sign at 10 of 11 KEY positions and follows which side's mission pays: the opponent's cards or primary pay for the charger's death on an objective or for the objective it stops contesting (s7469 Overwhelming Force with the target on nml_1; s7467 Vanguard Operation hold_min), or the decider's own primary loses an area (s7415 Death Trap); in the harmful ones the decider's mission pays (s7431 Assassination + OF on a 1-W character on the centre; s7403 Engage on All Fronts from the charge move). Add a term: expected VP for the decider (own active cards and primary: a kill of this target type where it stands, positions the charge move reaches) minus expected VP for the opponent (its active cards and primary: the charger's likely death where it will stand, the OC it gives up) minus own positional VP lost. The AI reads only its own cards today; reading the opponent's needs a check that 11e treats active cards as open information, and the deck order must never be read (rollouts replay it, so 's7457: the opponent draws Overwhelming Force next' is hidden - use draw odds). Merge check: a card-only proxy separates the twins and the 4 harmful positions but only 2 of 7 clear ones and flips 4 of 52 neutral rows (mean -0.47), so primaries and positions are required, and the term must be shown on the neutral set before it vetoes anything. Gate any charge-less effect to rounds 1-2; the bench fixtures carry 16-card secondary decks, so the term can fire there.
