### 1. OC-01 — `FIX_OBJ_AREA_REACH`

- **Title**: Reach/contest arithmetic measures unit centroid to the objective MARKER (3.79in), although every bench objective is a terrain area that one model controls by any base overlap
- **AI**: AIDecisionMaker.gd:8834-8855 (enemy/friendly_min_moves), 8913 (COORD-5 projected_enemy_oc reach), 8920 (RACE-1 e_moves), 8943-8949 (RACE-ARMY f_moves), 12654-12672 _reachable_contest_oc (used 8957-8959, drives enemy_weak/enemy_strong 8961-8981), 9700-9709 gap_in/turns_to_reach (feeds 9720-9724, 9748, 9835-9838, 9881-9887), 12577-12581 _capture_arrival. - **Engine**: MissionManager.gd:475-492 (host areas from source_pieces), 503-512 (_model_in_objective_range: any base overlap), 561-568 (model_gap_to_objective_range_px: base edge to area), 745-853 (_unit_oc_in_range per model). All 5 objectives in all 3 bench saves are terrain-hosted (area-large-1/2/13/14 7x11.5in; centre trapezoid-43/44 11.5x10in).
- **Rule**: 14.01 'that terrain area is the objective ... measure to and from the closest part of it'; 14.02 'A model is within range of a terrain objective while it is within that terrain area'; 01.04 / 01.04.01 (closest part of the base; one model is enough).
- **Fires/game**: Every Movement plan (~10+/game, all 3 fixtures, both seats): ~550-850 unit-objective terms/game, 40-60% with a wrong turn count at deployment (measured offline on decoded saves). Changed assignments: several per game (estimate, not measured).
- **VP effect**: Objectives look about one turn farther away: the AI under-counts enemy units that can step onto an objective next turn (under-defends) and under-counts its own contest capacity (abandons flippable objectives as enemy_strong). Estimate +0.5 to +2 VP/game if fixed (not measured). Largest systematic mismatch found.
- **Fix sketch**: New helper _obj_gap_px(unit, obj_pos, snapshot): when FIX_OBJ_AREA_REACH >= 0.5 and _held_dup_objective_at(snapshot, obj_pos) is non-empty, return the minimum over alive models of _held_dup_mm().model_gap_to_objective_range_px(model, entry) (0 when in range); otherwise the minimum over models of (base-edge distance to the marker - 3in), never the centroid. Cache per (unit, objective) per plan. Param 0 returns the old centroid arithmetic exactly. Print [OBJ_AREA_REACH] with old/new values whenever turns_to_reach or projected_enemy_oc changes (evaluation-level tag for the V2-1 fix gate). Implement first: OC-03 and OC-04 reuse the helper.
- **Test sketch**: mirror_custodes_2000_postdeploy: place a 5-model Custodian Guard with its front rank 5in from area-large-2's long edge and its centroid ~12in from the marker. Param 0: turns_to_reach 2 at M6 and the unit is missing from the opponent's projected_enemy_oc. Param 1: turns_to_reach 1 and it is counted.
